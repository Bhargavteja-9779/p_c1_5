# Claim–evidence audit (manuscript v1)

Claims checked: 106. I audited the Final_Manuscript.md build of 08:26. It was rebuilt while the audit ran: the 08:23–08:25 commits changed the Abstract, the NORMAN missing-descriptor paragraph and Data availability, and I checked the new text. All of about 180 inserted numbers were re-derived from metrics.json, statistics.json and the tables, and they match after rounding. The one exception is that medians of 130.5, 208.5 and 204.5 print as 130, 208 and 204.

Of the 106 claims listed, 55 are supported and 51 are not fully supported:

- **Contradicted (8):**
  - Table 1 'Median RT span' actually shows the median last-eluting RT (analyze.py:51).
  - '634 decoys in total' is a mean per system and draw.
  - 'Better than every fixed or residual-based tolerance at every n' fails at n=15 (±2·RMSE scores 10.60 vs 11.28 min).
  - 'All windows under-covered' with common calibrants ignores B6, which reached 0.902.
  - The 'late-eluting third of compounds' is the last third of the RT range, holding 16 % of test compounds.
  - The coverage-gap tests used the signed gap, not the absolute gap.
  - 'All RT data were taken from RepoRT' ignores the NORMAN data.
  - The Introduction says 'openly available', but Data availability says the code will be deposited upon acceptance.
- **Unsupported (3):**
  - The homoscedasticity explanation for short gradients.
  - The ionisation mechanism in the error analysis.
  - Peak memory 'dominated by the feature table'.
- **Overstated (11):**
  - 'Signature of heavy tails'.
  - Decoy results 'indistinguishable' from jackknife+, and parametric windows eliminating more 'only because' they kept fewer correct structures.
  - HILIC training-set causation, and validity 'does not depend' on base model or separation mode.
  - 'Reduced efficiency' on NORMAN with random calibrants (p = 0.32).
  - 'Not explained by' the missing descriptors (all three are missing together only in 32 PredRet systems).
  - 'No calibrant-only procedure'.
  - 'Pre-registered'.
  - 'Open implementation needs only RTs+SMILES'.
  - 'Would narrow'.
- **Partially supported (29):**
  - Undisclosed deviations from the plan: B6 lacks the planned nearest-neighbour term, the one-sided validity test was not run, and the gap test was signed.
  - The span–gain correlation is confounded by source group. Within folds ρ = −0.13 to −0.40, and the fold-adjusted ρ is −0.16 (my own calculation).
  - The SASC widths use a different statistic, so they conflict with Table S19 (3.86 and 4.60 min).
  - Bootstrap resamples were not all 10,000.
  - Flavonoids are omitted from the highest miss-rate classes.
  - Compute timings exclude featurisation, and the difficulty-model timing uses a smaller training set.
  - 'More than doubled' width: median width ×1.97.
  - The Abstract's 90.9–91.1 % range omits B6 (91.8 %).
